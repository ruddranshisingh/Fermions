Strings Attached Lab

A Kitaev chain hides one bit of information in two Majorana modes at its ends. To simulate it on a quantum computer you must first translate fermions into qubits, and there are several ways to do that. This lab shows how the choice of translation decides which qubit errors the topology shrugs off, which ones break it, and what each choice costs in gates.
